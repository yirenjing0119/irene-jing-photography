About Me

A portrait photographer with an eye for light. My interest in photography actually started at home — taking photos of my younger brother, chasing him around trying to catch the moments where he'd forget the camera was even there. That habit turned into a real passion for capturing people naturally rather than posed. Since then, my work has earned several awards through the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards, including a National Silver Medal along with multiple Gold and Silver Keys. I specialize in natural, outdoor photography — finding the moments, angles, and light that make a photo feel real. I shoot on a Fujifilm X-S10 and edit everything myself in Photoshop, always aiming for images that feel genuine, not overly polished. When I'm not behind the camera, I'm probably still thinking about the next shot I want to try.
