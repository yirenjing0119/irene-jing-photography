Outdoor Photography

I specialize in natural light, outdoor portrait photography — shot on location in Greater Boston in parks, city streets, and scenic spots that match your vibe. Whether you want candid, editorial-style shots or something more polished, I work with you to find light, backdrops, and moments that feel authentic. All sessions include professional editing, and I'm happy to talk through location and outfit ideas beforehand to make sure everything comes together.

Single Portrait Session

A one-on-one outdoor session built around you — your style, your energy, your story. We'll shoot at one or two locations that fit the mood you're going for, whether that's moody city streets, golden hour in a park, or somewhere more unexpected. I'll guide you through poses, angles, and outfit choices along the way. You'll walk away with a set of high-resolution, professionally edited images.

Multi-Person / Friends Session

Great for duo shoots, friend groups, or couples looking for candid and posed shots together outdoors. I focus on capturing genuine interaction and energy between you and the people you're with, not just stiff lineup shots. I can also help coordinate outfit colors and styles across the group so everyone looks cohesive. Ideal for anniversaries, friendship photos, or just wanting great photos of your people.

Family Session

A relaxed, outdoor family session designed to capture real connection — kids playing, parents laughing, the small moments in between the posed shots. I work around your family's pace, especially with younger kids, and prioritize natural, unforced photos over rigid formal ones. Happy to suggest coordinating (not matchy-matchy) outfit palettes ahead of time.

Kids Session

A fun, low-pressure outdoor session focused entirely on your kid's personality — playing, laughing, exploring, being themselves rather than posing stiffly. I move at their pace and capture genuine moments as they happen, which usually means way more relaxed (and better) photos than a traditional studio setup. Great for milestone shoots, birthdays, or just wanting some fresh photos of them outdoors. Happy to suggest simple, photogenic outfit ideas ahead of time too.
